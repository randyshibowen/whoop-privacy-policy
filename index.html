Privacy Policy — Personal WHOOP Training Integration

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This application is a personal, non-commercial integration intended for use by its owner to review and analyze their own fitness and wellness information. It is not offered as a public service.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the integration may access WHOOP data made available through the WHOOP API, including workouts, sleep, recovery metrics, and physiological or activity-related measurements, depending on granted permissions.

How information is used

Information is used only for personal fitness tracking, longitudinal analysis, and comparison with other training records. It is not used for advertising or sold.

Storage and service providers

Authorized fitness data may be stored in an access-controlled private GitHub repository for personal analysis. GitHub and WHOOP process data as service providers under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. The public repository hosting this privacy policy does not contain personal fitness records, credentials, or API tokens.

Security and retention

Access to stored data is restricted through account permissions. Authentication credentials and tokens are not intentionally published in source code. Records may be retained for the owner's personal historical analysis until deleted by the owner.

Control and revocation

The account owner can revoke the integration's access through applicable WHOOP account or connected-app controls and can delete stored records from their own repository. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored copies.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's functionality or data practices change.
